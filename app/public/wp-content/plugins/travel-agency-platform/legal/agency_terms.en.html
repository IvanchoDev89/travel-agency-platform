Agency. A verified natural or legal person that registers to list travel services and receive bookings (the «Agency»).

This agreement governs registration, listings, booking management, the economic model, data processing and termination. The Agency is the provider of the travel service and is solely liable to the traveler for its performance; the Platform provides technology, visibility, payment and support intermediary services.

2. Registration, verification and approval

The Agency provides identification, legal representation, contact, location, permits and insurance data and authorizes their verification. Until an administrator approves the Agency, it may prepare listings but they will not be visible to travelers or accept bookings. Approval is discretionary and based on legal and quality requirements. The Agency must keep its data current and notify changes in representation or licenses.

3. Agency obligations

  1. Publish truthful, complete and non-misleading information: prices, inclusions, exclusions, capacity, age or health restrictions, location and cancellation policies. Dark patterns and advertising that fails to disclose its advertising nature are prohibited.
  2. Hold all permits, licenses, registrations and insurance required by Costa Rican law for the activity and keep them valid.
  3. Honor advertised prices and availability, fulfill confirmed bookings and respond to requests within the stated period.
  4. Deliver the service with due care, personally or through authorized staff, and handle traveler claims through the enabled channels.
  5. Refrain from discrimination on grounds of nationality, sex, religion, disability or any other ground prohibited by law.
  6. Not use travelers obtained through the Platform to bypass commission or induce them to contract outside it, except operational coordination needed for the booked service.
  7. Comply with applicable tax, labor, consumer protection and data protection law.

4. Listings and content

The Agency retains ownership of its content and grants the Platform a non-exclusive, worldwide, free license to host, display, promote and technically adapt it while the relationship is in force. The Agency warrants it holds the necessary rights over photographs, music, trademarks and texts and will answer third-party claims. The Platform may remove unlawful or non-compliant content, notifying the reason.

5. Bookings, cancellations and disputes

The Agency must confirm or reject requests within the stated period and notify the traveler of material changes. Cancellations and refunds are governed by the policy published for each service, frozen at booking time. When a dispute is opened, the related commission is withheld until resolution. The Agency will cooperate with the Platform and the National Consumer Commission in handling claims.

6. Economic model

a) Commission. The Agency pays commission on the net service subtotal: the service price plus extras minus applied discounts. Commission is not calculated on the platform fee or the insurance premium. The applicable rate is determined, in order, by: (i) the subscribed plan rate; (ii) the Agency's own rate; (iii) the configured general commission; (iv) a default of ten percent (10%).

b) Volume incentive. From 11 paid bookings in a month the rate drops by 1 percentage point and from 31 by 2 points. The effective rate never falls below fifty percent (50%) of the Agency's base rate. Thresholds and reductions are configurable by the Platform and shown in the panel.

c) Platform fee. A charge to the Agency calculated on the subtotal as a configured percentage or fixed amount. It is not billed to the traveler or added to the advertised price.

d) Insurance. The travel insurance premium selected by the traveler is charged in full and excluded from the commission base. The Platform retains twenty percent (20%) of the premium for its intermediation, as disclosed.

e) Taxes. Each party is responsible for its own tax obligations. Electronic invoicing under the tax authority's regime is the responsibility of the party issuing the receipt under applicable law. Amounts in this agreement are without prejudice to legally due taxes.

7. Settlements

Commissions become payable when the booking is paid and not cancelled, refunded or disputed. The Agency may request settlement from its panel or the Platform may initiate it. Settlement is made by transfer or agreed means, withholding disputed bookings. If a payment fails it is recorded as failed and the Agency may request a new settlement. The Platform provides the detail of included bookings and amount.

8. Personal data protection

The Agency acts as controller of traveler data it collects to provide the service and as processor when handling data transmitted by the Platform for that purpose. It undertakes to process data under Law No. 8968 and its regulations, to respect information, access, rectification, update, erasure and objection (ARCO) rights, to apply technical and organizational security measures, not to transfer data to third parties without a legal basis and to promptly notify any security incident affecting data.

The Platform acts as controller of registration, browsing, payment and communication data under its Privacy Notice. Both parties will cooperate to handle ARCO requests and requirements from the Personal Data Protection Agency (PRODHAB).

9. Brand, advertising and affiliates

Use of the Platform's brand requires written authorization. The Agency may join affiliate and referral programs under published rules. Any paid recommendation, incentive, commission, promo code or affiliate link must be clearly and visibly identified as advertising or paid collaboration under Law No. 10946. The Agency is responsible for advertising it contracts or funds.

10. Confidentiality

The parties will keep confidential commercial, technical, pricing, commission and personal data accessed during the relationship, even after termination, unless legally required or written authorization is given.

11. Suspension and termination

The Platform may temporarily suspend access or post notices where there is risk to travelers, breach of this agreement, serious claims, payment anomalies or a legal requirement. Termination applies for repeated serious breach, fraud, loss of licenses or Agency request. On termination: (i) bookings and accrued obligations survive; (ii) undisputed payable commissions are settled; (iii) listings are removed; and (iv) data is handled under the Privacy Notice and the law.

12. Liability and indemnity

The Agency will indemnify the Platform for damages, sanctions and costs arising from its breaches, false information, unprovided services or third-party claims. The Platform's total liability to the Agency under this agreement is limited, except for intent or gross negligence, to commissions actually received in the twelve months preceding the event. Neither party is liable for lost profits.

13. Term, amendment and termination rights

This agreement has an indefinite term from digital acceptance. The Platform may propose new versions, which will be notified and require acceptance to continue operating; prior versions are retained with their hash. Either party may terminate with thirty (30) calendar days' notice, without prejudice to accrued obligations and pending bookings, which will be completed under the policy in force at booking time.

14. Governing law and jurisdiction

This agreement is governed by the laws of Costa Rica, in particular the Civil Code provisions on contracts and mandate, Law No. 7472, Law No. 8968, Law No. 8454 and Law No. 10946. The parties submit to the ordinary courts of San José, Costa Rica. Any arbitration must be agreed in writing later.
