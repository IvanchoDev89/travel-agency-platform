1. General principles

The policy applicable to a booking is the one published on the service page at the time of contracting and is frozen in the booking and invoice. That specific policy prevails over this general policy. Refunds are always calculated on the amount actually paid, capped at that amount and returned to the same payment method.

The platform fee and the Agency's commission relate to services cancelled by the traveler and are not returned. Where cancellation is due to Agency breach, force majeure or a Platform decision, section 6 applies.

2. Standard policies

  • Flexible. 100% refund up to 24 hours before the service starts.
  • Moderate. 100% refund up to 5 days before; 50% from 2 to 5 days; no refund within 48 hours.
  • Strict. 50% refund up to 7 days before; no refund within 7 days.
  • Non-refundable. No refund once the booking is confirmed and paid.

A service may define a more specific policy (for example by season or fare type). If the listing policy conflicts with this general policy, the listing prevails. If no policy is stated, the default by service type applies: flexible for accommodation and equipment, moderate for transport, strict for tours, boats and packages.

3. Right of withdrawal

Where the contract was concluded electronically or away from the business premises and the nature of the service permits it, the traveler may exercise the withdrawal right under Law No. 7472 within eight (8) days from perfection of the contract, without liability. For travel services with nearby dates, peak-season bookings or personalized services whose preparation cannot be reversed, the specific policy disclosed before payment applies. The Platform will interpret any doubt in favor of the consumer and facilitate exercise of the right from the booking detail.

4. How to request cancellation

The traveler must request cancellation from their bookings panel or booking page, optionally stating a reason. The request is recorded with date and time. For pending request-to-book reservations, cancellation is free and incurs no charges. If payment was captured, the Platform executes the refund under the policy and section 5.

5. Execution and refund timing

Where a refund is due, it is processed to the same payment method. Timing depends on the processor (typically 5 to 15 business days depending on the gateway and card issuer). If a refund fails, the booking is marked cancelled with refund pending and automatically retried; the traveler is notified. Partial refunds reduce the recorded paid amount and are reflected in the invoice and reports.

6. Cancellation by the Agency or force majeure

If the Agency cancels for reasons not attributable to the traveler, or the service cannot be provided due to force majeure, the traveler is entitled to a full (100%) refund of the amount paid, without prejudice to further liability under Law No. 7472. The Platform will help find an alternative but will not require the traveler to accept it.

7. No-show and changes

A no-show without prior cancellation is treated as a cancellation in the non-refundable period. Date or guest changes are handled with the Agency and subject to availability; if accepted, the original policy remains for the new date unless agreed otherwise in writing.

8. Travel insurance

If the traveler purchased travel insurance, the premium is refunded under the policy and the cause of cancellation. Claims for medical cancellation or other covered causes are submitted directly to the insurer; the Platform does not replace the insurer.

9. Complaints

Any dispute about application of this policy may be raised free of charge from the booking detail or user panel. The Platform will handle the case with the Agency and, failing agreement, the traveler may approach the National Consumer Commission (CNC) under Law No. 7472 and Law No. 10946.
